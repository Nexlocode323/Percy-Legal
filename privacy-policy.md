# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 27 August 2026

Percy ("the App") is a personal recipe manager and meal planner made by Harry Frith, an independent developer based in Australia. This policy explains what personal data the App collects, why, who else handles it, and what choices you have.

Questions about this policy or your data: **percysupport@gmail.com**.

## 1. What we collect and why

- **Your email address** — to create your account and sign you in.
- **Your password** — only if you sign up with email. It is stored only as a secure hash, never in a form we can read.
- **Your name and email from Apple or Google** — only if you choose to sign in that way, in place of a password. We never receive your Apple or Google password.
- **Your name and nationality** — asked at sign-up, used to personalise the App and to weight seasonal meal suggestions for the right hemisphere.
- **Contact details** — optional, so we can reach you if needed. Not required to use the App.
- **Your recipes** — the names, ingredients, steps, tags, ratings, and cook history you create or import. This is the core of the App.
- **Recipe photos you upload** — stored so the App can show them back to you.
- **What you import from** — a URL, pasted text, or a photo of a recipe. Used once to pull out the recipe details, then discarded; the resulting recipe is kept.
- **Meal-plan notes** — free text you write on the Plan tab, used once to turn your preferences into planning rules.
- **Push notification token** — only if you turn on notifications, so we can send reminders and household alerts to your device.
- **Subscription status** — for Premium subscribers only, so we know to unlock Premium features on your account.

We do **not** collect your location or your contacts, and we do not use any advertising or analytics tracking.

## 2. How we use it

Your data is used only to run the App: signing you in, storing and showing your recipes and meal plans, building shopping lists, and extracting recipe details when you use the import feature.

**We do not sell your personal data, and we will not.**

We may later add crash reporting to help us fix bugs — this would collect technical diagnostics only (device model, OS version, error details), never your recipes or account details. We may also introduce advertising to support a free tier. If we add advertising, we will update this policy first and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

## 3. Who else handles your data

We rely on a few service providers to run the App. None of them may use your data for their own purposes.

- **Supabase** — stores your account, recipes, and photos, and handles sign-in. [Privacy policy](https://supabase.com/privacy)
- **Anthropic** — when you import a recipe or write plan notes, the text or photo involved is sent to Anthropic's Claude AI to extract the recipe or your preferences. Nothing else about you is sent, and this data is not used to train Anthropic's models. [Privacy policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy)
- **TikTok** — if you import from a TikTok link, we ask TikTok's public service for the video's caption. No account data is sent.
- **Apple and Google** — if you sign in with them, they verify who you are and send us your name and email the first time. [Apple](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) · [Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- **Expo** — if you enable notifications, your device's notification token (nothing else) is sent to Expo so it can deliver the notification. [Privacy policy](https://expo.dev/privacy)
- **RevenueCat** — for Premium subscribers, manages the subscription and tells us your subscription status. It receives your purchase details and an anonymous ID for your account — never your name, email, or recipes. [Privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy)
- **Apple App Store / Google Play** — handle the payment itself if you subscribe. Neither we nor RevenueCat ever see or store your card or payment details.

Apart from these, your recipes, photos, and plans are stored only with Supabase and are shared with no one.

## 4. Recipe photos

A photo you attach to a recipe is stored in the cloud. It is not listed or linked publicly, but it is also not individually locked behind your login — someone with the exact link could open it. Please do not upload photos that show sensitive information in the background, such as faces, documents, or ID cards.

(This section is about photos you save to a recipe. Photos you use to *import* a recipe are covered in sections 1 and 3 — sent to our AI provider and not stored by us.)

## 5. Household sharing

You can form a household with other Percy users, such as family you live with. If you do, your recipes, meal plan, and shopping list become visible to — and editable by — the other members, and they can see your display name. This is the feature working as intended, not a data leak. Your personal recipe ratings, your login details, and your email are never shared with household members.

If you leave a household, the sharing stops going forward. Content you already added stays with the household rather than being deleted (section 7 covers what account deletion does).

## 6. Where your data is processed

Our providers (Supabase, Anthropic, Apple, Google, Expo, RevenueCat) may process and store data outside Australia, including in the United States. By using the App you consent to this. We rely on those providers' own safeguards for international transfers.

## 7. Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account and everything in it — recipes, photos, plan history — at any time from **Settings → Account → Delete Account**, or by emailing percysupport@gmail.com. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

If you have an active Premium subscription, deleting your account does not cancel it — cancel that separately in your Apple or Google subscription settings. We may keep minimal records where the law requires.

## 8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask us to: show you the data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, give you a portable copy, or stop certain uses of it. You can also complain to your local data protection authority. This applies wherever you are, including under the GDPR in Europe and the Privacy Act 1988 in Australia. To exercise any of these, email percysupport@gmail.com.

## 9. Children

The App is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has given us their data, contact us and we will delete it.

## 10. Security

We protect your data with standard measures, including encryption in transit and access rules that keep each account's data separate. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

## 11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Significant changes will be shown as a new effective date at the top of this page. Continuing to use the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.
