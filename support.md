# Percy — Support

**Last updated:** 7 September 2026

Percy is a personal recipe manager and meal planner made by Harry Frith, an
independent developer based in Australia. It lets you import recipes from a link,
pasted text, or a photo; auto-builds a week of dinners; turns that plan into an
aisle-sorted shopping list; and keeps ingredient amounts inline in each step
while you cook. You can also share one plan and list with the people you live
with.

## Get help

Email **percysupport@gmail.com** with your question or bug report. It helps to
include:

- your device and OS version (e.g. iPhone 14, iOS 18);
- what you expected to happen and what happened instead;
- the recipe or screen it happened on, if relevant.

This is a one-person project, so replies usually take **2–3 business days**.

## Common questions

### How do I delete my account?

In the app: **Settings → Delete account**. This permanently removes your recipes,
plan, shopping list, ratings, and cook history, and cancels the link between your
data and your sign-in. App Store subscriptions are managed by Apple and are not
cancelled by deleting your account — see below.

### How do I manage or cancel Percy Premium?

Premium is an auto-renewing subscription billed through your App Store account.
Manage or cancel it in **iOS Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions**. Cancelling
stops future renewals; the current period runs to its end. Deleting the app does
not cancel a subscription.

### I left a household and my recipes changed

Leaving a household is non-destructive: the shared plan, list, and any recipes
that stayed with the household are no longer visible to you, and you keep your
own independent copies of recipes you had added. Your personal display
preferences are restored. If something looks wrong after leaving, email us.

### An import came back empty or incomplete

Some sources don't publish machine-readable recipe data. Instagram links in
particular can't be read and will only fill in a name and link. For those, use
**paste text** or a **photo** of the recipe instead. You can always edit any
imported recipe by hand.

### Something else

Email **percysupport@gmail.com**.
